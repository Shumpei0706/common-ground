## Steps

1. **Create a room (browser A).** Open the app and choose **Create a new room**. Enter a room name, choose **How participants know each other**, set **Participants (3–6)**, set a **Host password** (10+ characters), and paste the key into **Host invitation key**. Press **Create room**.
2. **Invite.** In **Host view**, press **Copy participant link**.
3. **Join (browser B).** Open the copied link, enter a **Display name**, and press **Join with this name**. To test additional participants, use separate browser profiles or different browsers; private windows from the same browser profile can share cookies.
4. **Write a card.** Choose a category, enter one favorite work, add 1–10 short words (press Enter or comma after each), and press **Compare cards**.
5. **Optional Qloo prompts.** Press **Get related cards from Qloo**, then choose the exact work under **Which work did you mean?**. Feature tags and related works from two other domains appear. Use **Include this word** on feature cards or **Attach to final card** on related works. On this hosted review deployment, Qloo is armed through November 17, 2026, 15:00 JST (06:00 UTC) within finite quotas (2,000 cumulative calls including all historical calls, 200 per day, and 30 per room). No separate scheduling is needed while active, though availability is not guaranteed if a quota is reached, an upstream failure occurs, or the grant is disarmed; if Qloo is unavailable, continue with your own words or contact the team through Devpost.
6. **Publish.** Adjust your words under **Prepare your card for everyone** and press **Publish to everyone**. Until you publish, browser A sees only the participant's name and **Preparing (private)** status, never the work title or words.
7. **Facilitate (browser A).** After at least one participant has joined, press **Start discussion**, use **Highlight speaker**, and finish with **Close this room**.

Optional: in browser B, press **Unpublish card** and then **Edit card again** to return the card to editing; revise it and press **Publish to everyone** again.

Rooms expire two hours after creation.
